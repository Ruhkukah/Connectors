# T0 session rollover: proposed observation

Research only. This document starts nothing and changes no deadline or release pin.

The current [MOEX T0 schedule](https://www.moex.com/s438), checked4November2026, separates15:00 settlement clearing from16:00 mark-to-market clearing. Evening trading runs14:00–16:00; the next ordinary morning starts07:00. The [2April2026 notice](https://www.moex.com/n98981/?nt=0) announced the same weekday timings effective1April. Its weekend wording is older than the current schedule; use the current schedule and any later owner-supplied outage notice.

The [SPECTRA Plaza2 manual](https://ftp.moex.com/pub/ClientsAPI/Spectra/CGate/test/docs/p2gate_ru.html) distinguishes a Trading Day identifier (sess_id) from intraday trading-period/state changes. A settlement event alone is not evidence of a new identifier. Neither schedule specifies the exact second at which T0 publishes the new sess_id; do not promise that16:00 itself changes it. Publication/loading of the next day's data may occur after clearing or at next startup. No date-specific notice proving that timing has been located.

Propose a separately authorized **Thursday5November15:50–16:15MSK**, read-only, one warm-router client. This deliberately crosses the scheduled16:00 mark-to-market boundary and requires a new explicit stop deadline beyond the former15:45 limit. Record committed session/session_state identifiers and states, source session authority, sys_events session_data_ready/clearing events, LifeNum, ClearDeleted and snapshot/ONLINE; no orders or other clients. If the host closes or no new session is published, report unavailable/not observed, not a failed assumption about session numbering. No automatic reopen loop beyond reviewed recovery policy.

If a continuous transition is unavailable, propose a second separately authorized read-only capture after confirmed system startup on the next ordinary trading morning (about07:05–07:15MSK, after a60s warm-router wait). Comparing closing and next-start identifiers can prove a change occurred between captures; it cannot locate its precise time or prove uninterrupted rollover recovery. New-session order-entry testing remains a different owner-authorized block with the full gate. Do not infer monotonic+1 numbering, use the actual committed identifier, and do not mix resting-order sessions into session authority by assumption.
