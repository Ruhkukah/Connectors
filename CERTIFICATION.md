# MoexConnector 1.0.0 — CGate certification

The first certificate covers own-account SPECTRA trading over CGate 9.9. It includes TRADE, POS, PART, REFDATA, USERORDERBOOK, AGGR20, SESSIONSTATE and INSTRUMENTSTATE. Declare AddOrder, DelOrder, DelUserOrders and MoveOrder. TWIME, FullOrderLog, public DEALS, Iceberg commands, DelOrdersByBFLimit, COD, RFS, .NET/C ABI and DTC order entry are excluded. Public DEALS remains an optional read-only feature, off by default; its outstanding defects are deferred. Deferred source is preserved at `archive/pre-cgate-certification-remediation-20260930`.

Automated tests demonstrate implementation behavior. They do not certify a release or replace the full MOEX test day. The internal live rehearsal and MOEX scenarios below still require the actual router, vendor CGate, provisioned test account and agreed test schedule. No broker command is authorized merely by running the build or tests.

The official [certification procedure](https://www.moex.com/files/4xgv6e2x1paqr1zkn2fmq093cj), [technical requirements](https://www.moex.com/files/41w8g1tt63pd9tq9drmk4n3g4z) and [CGate manual](https://ftp.moex.com/pub/ClientsAPI/Spectra/CGate/prod/docs/p2gate_en.pdf) govern the live rehearsal. Historical source and process documents remain at the archive tag.

## Questionnaire answers

- Product/version: MoexConnector 1.0.0. Developer, certificate holder, contact details and legal declarations remain owner-supplied.
- Purpose: own trading only. Item 1h: no broker/customer connections to the certified trading driver; optional local DTC visualization is a separate read-only interface. Item 1n: market data for own trading. Item 1p: deployment for own use; no broker/client service declared. Legal distribution rights are a separate owner answer.
- Direct gateway language: C++20. One TCP CGate connection while the optional DTC runner is stopped (required during certification); one owner thread uses the connection, listeners and publisher. The driver rejects calls from other threads before querying CGate.
- Connection: `p2tcp://<configured-router>;app_name=<stable-instance-id>;timeout=2000`. Specify the actual deployment address/instance in the submitted form.
- Trading listeners: `p2repl://FORTS_TRADE_REPL`, `p2repl://FORTS_USERORDERBOOK_REPL`, `p2repl://FORTS_POS_REPL`, `p2repl://FORTS_PART_REPL`, `p2repl://FORTS_REFDATA_REPL`, `p2repl://FORTS_AGGR20_REPL`, `p2repl://FORTS_SESSIONSTATE_REPL`, `p2repl://FORTS_INSTRUMENTSTATE_REPL`. Initial open settings are `mode=snapshot+online`. TRADE waits for committed POS.info and a completed USERORDERBOOK publication from the same TRADE LifeNum, then opens with `lifenum`, `rev.user_deal` and `rev.heartbeat`. `orders_log` has no revision floor, preserving the full current-life order history for reconstruction. When OPEN confirms the requested LifeNum without a LifeNum notification, the requested epoch is adopted; an earlier server LifeNum notification takes precedence. Read-only DTC and the trading profile use server replication schemes; required field names and types are validated at OPEN.
- Publisher: `p2mq://FORTS_SRV;category=FORTS_MSG;name=<instance>;timeout=60000;scheme=|FILE|<scheme-dir>/forts_messages.ini|message`. Reply listener: `p2mqreply://;ref=<instance>`.
- Polling: process up to 100 zero-timeout calls or 5ms, then service commands, kill/input and other owner work; wait at most 50ms when idle.
- REPLSTATE is received and logged, but is not retained as a reopen cursor. Each replication reopen requests `mode=snapshot+online`; TRADE uses the committed POS.info LifeNum and trade revision for `user_deal` and `heartbeat`. A completed same-epoch USERORDERBOOK publication supplies the reconstruction barrier, rather than an `orders_log` revision floor. Accepted-epoch OPEN followed by ONLINE supplies the catch-up proof, including an empty own account. Ordinary periodic USERORDERBOOK publications do not reopen TRADE. Listener URLs use exchange-supplied replication schemes, so no client replication alias is selected. Replay-key effectiveness remains a native qualification check.
- Futures only: REF metadata must identify a regular future with `is_spread=false`. Calendar spreads, options and multileg order entry are refused; cancellation remains available.
- Automatic recovery cancellation is account/ISIN/ext_id-scoped after an unresolved Add timeout or a tracked non-Move order missing from a committed LifeNum/full-clear reload snapshot and a mature TRADE watermark. Ordinary reconnects and delta overflow do not start missing-order cancellation. A Move timeout/system100 does not initiate DelUserOrders. A missing unresolved Move after history loss requires operator reconciliation.
- Interpretation: **Анализ схемы**. Required field names/types are checked at OPEN; compatible additions are ignored.
- CGate `t` / P2TIME calendar components use Moscow time (UTC+03), as confirmed by MOEX; the native decoder normalizes them to UTC independent of host timezone. The UTC `moment_ns` value is retained without a timezone shift. This is distinct from UDP FAST/SIMBA, which are outside this certificate.
- Message creation: **Сообщения создаются перед отправкой и уничтожаются после отправки**.
- Commands/replies use the fixed `forts_messages.ini|message` layout. Native message names/types and payload sizes are checked before submission and reply decoding; this is separate from server replication scheme binding. AddOrder/179, DelOrder/177, DelUserOrders/186, MoveOrder/176; system messages 99 and 100; timeout processing. Type 1 is a day limit order, type 2 is IOC.
- Market validity starts with a fresh complete snapshot followed by ONLINE, as confirmed by MOEX for late start/restart. A snapshot session_data_ready row is an annotation, not a required new barrier. A later committed online event_type 5 revokes authority; pruning that event or repeating ONLINE cannot restore it. Order readiness uses private stream and session/instrument states independently of AGGR. No fixed session ID is configured.
- Instrument status is bound to the session from committed `FORTS_INSTRUMENTSTATE_REPL.sys_events.sess_id`, as advised by MOEX, and corroborated by current REFDATA. Session rollover requires fresh status; resets and deletion of that session proof revoke trading authority until it is restored.
- Opening auction: instrument public_state 6 permits Day limit Adds. IOC/FOK and Move are refused, following CGate Appendix C codes 4302 and 4300. Close-only states 8/9 are not declared for new entry. Cancels remain available in non-entry phases (0/2/5/6/8/9).
- Declare morning/day/evening support as implemented; attach the full-day logs before claiming it was rehearsed. Leave FullOrderLog/public DEALS/COD/RFS unchecked.

## Operator emergency procedure

Keep an owner-managed deployment card beside the terminal containing the broker desk's telephone and authenticated emergency channel, the TEST login and section, broker/client identity, instance ID, router endpoint, dated interaction journal, identity file, absolute CGate `[p2syslog] logfile` and router log location. Fill these private values before the rehearsal; keep them outside Git. MOEX TEST technical support is [help@moex.com and +7 495 363-32-32](https://www.moex.com/s3439).

1. Send `kill on` through the command socket and inspect `status`. Identify each configured instrument, working order and current POS.
2. Send `cancel-all ISIN` for each affected instrument. Review any skipped client_order_ids in its result and journal; these orders were not cancelled. Check per-order `instance_owned` in `status` and reconcile skipped unresolved/conflicted orders through the emergency channel. Keep the driver polling; verify accepted replies and committed TRADE terminal evidence. A disconnect or forced quit does not cancel venue orders.
3. If cancellation cannot be confirmed, contact the broker desk through its authenticated emergency channel to establish venue orders and positions and request risk reduction. Escalate CGate, router or TEST session failures to MOEX support with the instance, section, time and sanitized error; never send credentials or keys.
4. Preserve the interaction journal, identity file and CGate/router logs. Repair the fault, reconnect through the product, reconcile fresh TRADE/USERORDERBOOK/POS, and confirm flatness before any new rehearsal entry. Archive native logs immediately after the session because vendor rotation is bounded.

The interaction journal is append-only. Do not use copytruncate, replace an active journal or rotate it while the driver runs. Low free space enables cancel-only protection while interaction logging continues if the writer is healthy. After repairing storage, `storage ok` verifies free space and durably flushes the journal/checkpoint before leaving protection; the kill switch stays on until an explicit `kill off`.

## Full-day rehearsal

Load account values and the CGate software key from an owner-managed environment file with permissions `0600`,
then run `set -a; . "$MOEX_ENV_FILE"; set +a`. The file must define `MOEX_BROKER`, `MOEX_CLIENT`,
`MOEX_LOGIN`, `MOEX_EXT_ID_RANGE`, `MOEX_PLAZA2_CGATE_SOFTWARE_KEY` and the following literal template (substitute a readable absolute ini path):

```sh
MOEX_CGATE_ENV='ini=/absolute/path/client.ini;log=p2:p2syslog;key=${MOEX_PLAZA2_CGATE_SOFTWARE_KEY}'
```

The driver expands the software-key placeholder internally. Do not put the key on the command line or commit
the environment file. Router login credentials belong in the router configuration; the driver's unused
`--credentials-env` value is optional. The configured CGate software key remains required.

Run the qualifying dry run and full-day rehearsal on a regular weekday T1 session. Complete the exchange-action safety fixes before further TEST orders and the remaining reliability fixes before the full-day rehearsal. Resolve ALRS-12.26 and CNY-12.26 IDs from fresh committed REFDATA. Before the first order, confirm own POS is flat and no working/unresolved orders exist, then print the effective configuration. Compute each quote-notional cap from the REF current price at no more than two contracts; the global five-open-order cap also bounds each instrument.

For native timing qualification, add `--measure-timings` to `plaza2 run`. Each poll collects bounded stream/table/event counts; only slow eventful turns or slow owner work enter the journal. `slow_owner_poll` separates the vendor process call (including decoding, projection and any blocking wait), the journal observer, order-state work, flush and dispatch. Blocking process time is not a pure idle-wait measurement. `command_timing` marks the actual vendor post call and the immediate, unqueued `place()` to post interval; queued orders do not receive an immediate-latency label. For host clock evidence, compare a live row's UTC `moment_ns` with its matching local receipt or journal append time, excluding snapshot/replay rows. A stream-wide last-callback time beside a book snapshot is not a matched-row clock sample.

Observe the read-only run for at least 30 minutes: all listeners ONLINE, first TRADE replay revisions against the committed anchors, journal growth per hour, CPU, owner-thread stall p99, USERORDERBOOK republish timing and the earlier key-check failure's cause. Preserve committed POS.info `trades_rev`/`trades_lifenum` changes alongside own POS `xpos`, side and day-open quantities, `last_deal_id`, and own user_deal source revisions. Continue this trace across an authorized weekday fill before declaring position coverage verified. A cutoff change alone does not release reservations; committed position quantities and exact trade evidence must prove coverage. Keep the native trace on the TEST host and report the conclusion in the PR.

The 7 October 2026 read-only TEST observation recorded 11 completed USERORDERBOOK publications with a median interval of 179.984 seconds (approximately 180 seconds). This is an observed cadence for that run, not a guaranteed exchange interval or full trading qualification. Any timeout waiting for the next completed USERORDERBOOK publication must exceed 180 seconds with an explicit transport/replay margin; do not rely on the two-minute interval described in the manual alone. Elapsed time does not replace committed publication completion and matching-epoch checks. Reply, cancellation and shutdown deadlines remain separate from this periodic snapshot cadence.

Build Release on the actual Linux deployment host, then run the exact build with the provisioned TEST account. Preserve default router/client logging. Set the instance name, router, instrument list, rate and risk limits explicitly. Example configuration (environment variables carry local settings and account values):

```sh
MOEX_DAY_LOG="logs/moex_connector-$(date +%Y%m%d).ndjson"
build/apps/moexctl plaza2 run \
  --runtime-root /opt/moex/cgate --scheme-dir /opt/moex/cgate/scheme \
  --config-dir /opt/moex/cgate/config --env-settings-var MOEX_CGATE_ENV \
  --broker-code-env MOEX_BROKER --client-code-env MOEX_CLIENT \
  --login-env MOEX_LOGIN --ext-id-range "$MOEX_EXT_ID_RANGE" \
  --router 127.0.0.1:4101 --instance-id moex_certification \
  --environment test --isin-id <ALRS-current-ISIN> --isin-id <CNY-current-ISIN> --allow-orders --sole-instance \
  --max-commands-per-second 5 --max-quantity 2 --max-open-orders 5 \
  --max-notional <ALRS-current-ISIN>=<ALRS-quote-cap> \
  --max-notional <CNY-current-ISIN>=<CNY-quote-cap> \
  --max-position <ALRS-current-ISIN>=2 --max-position <CNY-current-ISIN>=2 --log "$MOEX_DAY_LOG" \
  --state logs/moex_certification.state
```

Measure clock offset with the host's time service; pass `--clock-offset-us` with that measurement. A null/unavailable startup value is not clock qualification. Use `config/cgate.ini` as the logging template: enable `log=p2:p2syslog` in the environment settings or the `[cgate]` section, and set `[p2syslog] logfile` to an absolute writable path for the client log. Omit severity overrides to preserve CGate's default debug level. Preserve the router INI logging defaults. Archive client and router logs immediately after the session, before the vendor's hourly rotation and 168-file retention remove earlier evidence.

1. Leave the connector idle for five minutes.
2. Stop/start the router; interrupt/restore its upstream connection. Verify commands stop during loss, the process waits, and private streams/commands resume after ONLINE.
3. Place at least three simultaneous orders. Partially fill one; cancel another; move one. Exercise DelUserOrders through `cancel-all ISIN` as well as DelOrder.
4. Restart the process with two working orders. Use `status` to obtain their recovered client IDs, cancel one, and place a new order. Verify remaining quantities against TRADE.
5. Submit a burst above the selected rate. Verify all queued commands eventually send and every rolling one-second window respects the cap; exercise a flood penalty and cancel retry.
6. Continue through intermediate clearing, main clearing and the evening session without restarting. Verify the new `sess_id` is followed automatically and repeat every declared command.
7. Arrange TCS restart/reload and reserve-switch scenarios with the MOEX specialist. Confirm TRADE replay uses the committed POS and completed same-epoch USERORDERBOOK revision anchors; do not guess new replay keys without observing 9.9 behavior.
8. Keep the CGate client log, router log and application NDJSON. Run `python3 scripts/reconstruct_orders.py "$MOEX_DAY_LOG"` to reconstruct each order's command, reply, trade and replication lifecycle. Agree the formal MOEX day/schedule after the internal rehearsal.

## Administration and emergency procedure

Run one host per stable instance ID. Assign a positive inclusive `MIN:MAX` ext_id range below INT32_MAX to each instance; ranges must not overlap. By default, `cancel-all` and signal shutdown target this instance only: submitted orders or recovered orders whose login and ext_id range match. Known IDs use DelOrder; unresolved owned IDs use ext_id-scoped DelUserOrders. Identity-conflicted unresolved orders require operator reconciliation and are not cancelled automatically. Explicit `cancel ID` targets a known venue order_id when available. If an unresolved Add has conflicting ext_id evidence and no known order_id, it refuses cancellation with "identity conflict: resolve via broker/exchange, order_id unknown"; establish the exact identity through the broker/exchange emergency channel. Set `MOEX_EXT_ID_RANGE` to that assignment and preserve its identity checkpoint. The log and identity state are exclusively locked. The state defaults to `<log-directory>/<instance-id>.state`; keep the same `--state FILE` across log rotation or directory changes. Each instance durably reserves blocks of 1000 ext_id and user_id values before handing out identifiers from memory. Only block exhaustion rewrites the reservation checkpoint; restart skips unused values from the previous block. Interaction records are flushed on the owner loop every 250ms and at shutdown, without a per-command journal sync. A valid checkpoint skips the durable log prefix and recovers only its tail. Missing legacy state requires a one-time log scan; damaged state refuses restart. Preserve both files and reconcile before restoring a backup. Retain vendor logging defaults and keep secrets in local configuration. Historical evidence bundles and deferred source are preserved at the archive tag.

The `run` command owns CGate for the entire day. Enter `place ID ISIN buy|sell QTY PRICE [day|ioc]`, `cancel ID`, `move ID QTY PRICE`, `cancel-all ISIN`, `kill on|off`, `status` or `quit`. Move quantity is the logical order's total quantity including confirmed fills; regime 3 excludes fills racing the move. Executions come from deduplicated own user_deal rows, not quantity-minus-rest. Recovered orders with incomplete historical fills permit cancellation but refuse Move. `--allow-orders` requires an explicit rate, login, deployment-assigned nonoverlapping `ext_id` range, quantity/open-order limits and a quote-notional and position cap for every target ISIN. Risk includes all own recovered working orders: outstanding quote notional is capped per instrument, while signed committed own POS, own fills not yet reflected in POS, and same-side working quantity are checked without netting opposite-side orders. Move reserves the largest old, proposed or uncertain replacement exposure. Quote notional is not margin. Expired/nonmember instruments refuse new orders. Console and command-channel Add/Move carry manual-entry compliance_id M. The recovered: client-ID prefix is reserved for reconstruction. An oversized input line reports an error and discards that line while later commands remain available.

Before sending orders, inspect `status`: it shows configured order/rate/risk limits, connection/publisher/listener states, committed snapshot readiness, recovery details and pinned schema incompatibility. State transitions print to stderr. The journal names streams/tables and records actual post attempts, operator input and local refusals. Startup records the source revision when available and executable SHA-256. Committed exchange/NCC messages print to stderr and the journal; `status` retains the last 20.

Terminal disconnects, SIGHUP and broken output pipes leave the host running. Stdin EOF is retried; an incomplete final line is refused and audited. The owner thread also accepts commands through an owner-only UNIX socket, defaulting to `<log-file>.sock`; select a shorter explicit path with `--command-socket PATH` when necessary. Reconnect with `moexctl plaza2 cmd --log "$MOEX_DAY_LOG" "status"`, or use the same explicit socket path. The same channel accepts `kill on` and `cancel-all ISIN`. Requests carry a nonce and deadline; expired requests and extra commands in a connection are refused. The client checks the echoed nonce and exits nonzero on refusal. Only one host may own an endpoint. Startup removes only a same-owner socket verified stale by a refused connection; other existing endpoints are refused before CGate login.

Known relist limitation: an old-ID deletion and a next-session `id_ord1` row can arrive in separate TRADE transactions. CLI status and the journal can show `Cancelled` between commits. The later same-account row restores the logical order when it links the prior ID, matches ISIN/direction, and increases `sess_id`. During a session transition, wait for complete current-session TRADE/USERORDERBOOK reconciliation and confirm venue state before interpreting terminal status or placing a replacement.

Old terminal orders are pruned once their exchange identity is resolved. An unresolved Add retains its submitted request and reply correlation across session changes, including after an absence result, so a late accepted 179 can restore tracked exposure and cancellation intent. A timed-out Move likewise retains its possible replacement exposure until authoritative identity/terminal evidence resolves it.

An unresolved order or cancellation requiring operator action blocks Add and Move on its affected ISIN; other instruments retain their normal readiness and risk checks. The uncertain exposure and warning remain visible. Establish the exact venue identity through committed replication or the broker emergency channel, then explicitly cancel the tracked order with a known order_id and wait for native terminal proof. An identity-conflicted order with unknown order_id refuses `cancel ID`; resolve it through the broker/exchange emergency channel rather than cancelling by ext_id. For an unresolved instrument-wide cancellation, deliberately renew the scope and obtain accepted 186 plus a later committed TRADE view. Do not clear uncertainty by editing the checkpoint or submitting a replacement Add. The kill switch still blocks new risk on every instrument.

Private delta overflow or a TRADE/USERORDERBOOK disconnect triggers current USERORDERBOOK + TRADE snapshot reconciliation before new entries resume. The host logs `private_history_gap`, stays running and retains order identities, command correlations and lifetime trade deduplication. A plain Close/reopen, including unchanged TRADE purge-floor markers without row retirement, preserves tracked fill baselines and deduplicates the replayed user_deal rows, so established orders can still Move. An advancing committed regular TRADE order/deal purge floor, actual regular own-row retirement, a changed TRADE LifeNum or delta overflow marks existing exchange orders and already-posted unresolved Adds as lacking a complete fill baseline: they permit cancellation but refuse Move. Multileg-only table clears retain their private projection history-loss signal but do not invalidate regular-order fill baselines, because this order path excludes multileg orders and fills. An unsent Add posted after the recovery barrier starts a fresh tracked fill baseline; a late 179 cannot restore a baseline invalidated by subsequent history loss. Reconstruction drains deltas while waiting for completed same-epoch USERORDERBOOK and anchored TRADE ONLINE; healthy periodic book publications do not repeat that barrier.

For an incident, enter `kill on` to stop new orders, then `cancel-all ISIN` for each configured instrument. The kill switch permits risk-reducing cancels. Confirm terminal order states from TRADE and positions from POS; a successful cancel reply alone is not final proof. If connectivity is down, the process keeps waiting and retains queued cancels. Use the broker/exchange emergency channel when venue state cannot be established. A process stop or socket disconnect is not exchange cancel-on-disconnect.

Ordinary `quit` refuses shutdown while orders are working. `quit --force` drains already-requested cancellations for up to 10 seconds. In trading mode, SIGINT/SIGTERM enables the kill switch and requests instance-scoped cancel-all on every configured instrument, then waits up to 10 seconds for cancellation and terminal order evidence. Only `--sole-instance` permits instrument-wide DelUserOrders, including manual and other-instance orders on that client code. The single-instance certification example sets this flag; use it only for that declared exclusive setup. The journal records the drain result; shutdown reports any outstanding orders. The first signal always starts a fresh 10-second deadline, including after an earlier quit request. A second SIGINT or SIGTERM ends the drain immediately and reports outstanding orders. An incomplete or timed-out drain exits with status 8; a host-stop failure exits with status 7. A read-only run sends no commands.

The journal and identity filesystems require at least 64 MiB available at startup; capacity is checked again every minute. A storage failure alerts on stderr, latches `cancel_only` in status and keeps CGate polling and order/fill reconciliation alive. Add, Move and `kill off` are refused. DelOrder and DelUserOrders may continue only with IDs from the already durable reservation block; its exhaustion blocks further posts. Ordinary events stop buffering after an actual writer failure, so unavailable storage cannot accumulate an unbounded interaction queue. A capacity-only guard continues logging while the writer remains healthy. Logging may be incomplete after a writer failure. Use the broker/exchange emergency channel when cancellations cannot be confirmed, repair storage and reconcile before restarting. Use a dated `--log` each day and keep the same per-instance `--state`; never use logrotate `copytruncate` or truncate an active log. Archive it after its owner stops.

Explicit shutdown and fatal non-storage startup/poll failures close CGate, then attempt to flush the journal. Working orders may remain. Shutdown prints outstanding order IDs and states to stderr with that warning. After restart, recover IDs from USERORDERBOOK and TRADE before acting. Before an Add reply or timeout, its order_id stays 0 and own replicated candidates remain separately recovered, conservatively exposed. After timeout, an Unknown unresolved Add can adopt committed replication only when login, full account, ext_id, submitted session, ISIN, side, numeric price and requested quantity match. A late conflicting 179 keeps it Unknown and requires operator action. Unresolved recovery uses account/ISIN/ext_id-scoped DelUserOrders with the valid both-sides selector. Other own IDs and fills remain separate. Established IDs change only through Move reply linkage, validated native prevorder_id linkage after Move timeout, or documented id_ord1 relisting. Never replace an Add merely because its reply timed out. After three definitive cancellation business rejections, status exposes `operator_action_required`; establish venue state and use the emergency channel before deliberately retrying. A renewed cancel, accepted reply or relisted ID retains that order's warning until native terminal proof; order-specific warnings clear only after native terminal proof. An unresolved broad-cancel gate and alert remain even if all currently known orders terminate; later rejections cannot settle an earlier uncertain request. Deliberately renew that scope and obtain an accepted 186 followed by a later TRADE commit before admitting new risk. Flood penalties and system100 do not spend that business-rejection limit. Known-ID cancels retry with pacing; an unacknowledged DelUserOrders has a separate three-uncertain-outcome limit (timeouts or system100) and then requires operator action.
