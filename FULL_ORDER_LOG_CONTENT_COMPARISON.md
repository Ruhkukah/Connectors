# Order-content comparison probe

The composite p2ordbook listener can consume snapshot info internally without exposing its rows to the application. Consequently ONLINE is book-completeness authority, but it is not an application-visible revision anchor. The probe retains its older exact-cutover counters when genuine anchors exist; it no longer depends on them for the new content observations.

For each tracked ISIN, compare canonical sets keyed by public_order_id, with remaining quantity, exact integer price, side and session. Matching price-level aggregates alone are insufficient: two different orders at the same level must differ. Snapshot replID is an administrative row identity, not the public order ID.

The composite is sampled after ONLINE or TN_COMMIT and may be resampled when a raw publication completes. Resampling reads the latest committed idle image, not pending orders: inspection requires a valid book, no active transaction and no dirty levels. This also lets a quiet committed book be compared after the raw listener retires its previous publication cohort. Diagnostic inspection does not run on the normal writer publication path. The raw listener is sampled only after a completed publication_state=1 info transaction, including the valid empty set. Its capture interval begins at the first transaction since the preceding completed publication.

A raw set may match a composite committed state whose local residency overlaps the raw capture interval plus a fixed two-second grace on either side. Quiet committed states remain current until replaced or invalidated. A raw snapshot arriving first waits for a later composite commit within the grace period. The tolerance is local monotonic arrival time, not an invented exchange revision or timestamp. Matching is per ISIN, not a claim of an atomic multi-instrument snapshot.

Results distinguish equal_within_arrival_window, different_within_arrival_window and unresolved (no committed sample, lifecycle retirement, bounded-history eviction or pending observations at stop). Differences are diagnostic candidates, not proof of exchange-revision inconsistency; an actual feed delay can exceed the tolerance. Neither equality nor a zero difference count proves simultaneous cuts. Every record says same_revision_proven=false. The final report retains p9_qualified=false and complete=false, and exit3 for inconclusive qualification (exit4 if differences are observed).

History is bounded to4096 fingerprints per ISIN and4096 pending raw/ISIN observations. Lost history cannot be converted into a definite difference. Lifecycle controls retire both histories. A currently incomplete/invalid composite cannot be sampled. Size, schema, framing, admission and private-order gates are unchanged. The live comparison needs a separately reviewed and pinned release; implementing this probe does not deploy or start it.

On a no-match report, diagnostics select the retained committed image nearest the raw publication's completion (ties prefer the earlier image). missing_orders counts raw IDs absent from composite; extra_orders counts composite IDs absent from raw; changed_orders counts common IDs with a different remaining quantity, price, side or session. Only counts are emitted, never IDs or values. With no retained image, counts are null rather than fabricated zeros. nearest_sample_in_window explicitly identifies an outside-window nearest image. composite_commits_in_window counts observed native publications inside the raw capture interval expanded by the tolerance; idle resampling is excluded. Retired/evicted history makes this a retained-observation count, not proof that every exchange revision was captured.

Immutable identical order images are shared. Retained raw and composite images have a combined one-million-order-entry storage budget as well as the fingerprint/pending limits. Exhaustion retires affected observations explicitly as unresolved_order_storage_limit; a single image exceeding the bound is refused. Diagnostic state cannot grow without bound to supply difference counts.
